## Description

In this role, you will be responsible for developing all facets of a solution to streamline the work of internal Apple Store Online teams. You will utilize all aspects of your technical skillset, from cutting edge front-ends and back-ends to maintaining and improving our world-class AliCloud infrastructure. Our ideal candidate must have demonstrated expertise in Node.js, Angular, Typescript, JavaScript, PostgreSQL and Cloud environments like AliCloud.

Typical day-to-day responsibilities include:

- Developing and owning all aspects of front-end including UI/UX, back-end, database of applications
- Oversee and maintain cloud hosting infrastructure like AliCloud
- Formulate testing strategy and high-impact unit and E2E tests to streamline future updates and ensure reliability
- Maintain a flexible work schedule in production support scenarios and to collaborate with team members and stakeholders in the AMR region.

## Responsibilities

- Ensure infrastructure and applications meet strict data and privacy legal requirements.
- Partner with stakeholders to define requirements and technical feasibility of new solutions
- Create agentic workflows that accelerate development and continuously improve quality code and infrastructure.
- Implement enhancements in production applications to meet evolving business requirements

## Minimum Qualifications

- 7+ years of experience and high degree of proficiency developing software using following technologies: Angular or React, NestJS or similar back-end framework, Node.js, SQL
- Administration of infrastructure using cloud technologies such as AliCloud, container technologies such as Docker or Kubernetes, and CI/CD and DevOps practices
- Strong problem-solving and analytical skills, and experience applying these in both new development settings as well as under high-impact production support conditions
- 1-2 years of experience with Generative AI
- Experience developing RESTful APIs
- BA/BS degree or 8+ years professional experience in a directly relatable role

## Preferred Qualifications

- 8 or more years of experience and high degree of proficiency
- Strong UI/UX design skills
- Proficient at distilling stakeholder feedback and challenges into actionable requirements
- Proficient at updating frameworks, npm packages ensuring reliability and security
- 2 or more years of experience and proficiency with multiple Generative AI, with experience embedding Generative AI skills and agents directly in pipelines for automatic PR creation and human-in-the-loop development flows.
