For the Equip Coding Assessment, we evaluate candidates on their overall engineering quality rather than just whether they arrive at the correct solution. Common reasons candidates don't progress include:

Code quality and maintainability not meeting the expected standard.
Problem-solving approach lacking structure or robustness.
Implementation not demonstrating strong production engineering practices.
Technical depth not aligned with the level of the role.
In some cases, the assessment identifies behaviour that violates the assessment guidelines or raises integrity concerns, and those candidates are not progressed.
